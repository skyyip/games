    02 / THREE LITTLE SECONDS

    Look. Remember.
    Draw it right there.

    Trace the outline once in its original place. You can undo a stroke or clear your drawing before submitting.

    Keyboard: focus the board. Arrow keys move the pen; Shift moves faster. Space lifts or lowers the pen. Use the submit button when finished.

    How your memory is scored
    1. Everyone sees the same randomly generated outline in the same position, for three seconds each.
    2. After it disappears, redraw its outline in the original place. Position, size and shape all matter.
    3. The overlap percentage combines how much of the original outline you covered and how much of your drawing follows that outline. A small tolerance allows natural finger wobble.
    4. Extra scribbles lower accuracy. A blank drawing cannot be submitted. Undo and Clear are available before submitting.
    5. The highest percentage wins. Scores are compared to one decimal place; equal percentages share the win.
    6. Drawings and scores stay hidden until everyone has finished. A new round creates a new shape.

    Leaving the screen during the three-second look hides the shape immediately. It cannot be revealed again during that turn.

    About Memory Trace / 玩法介紹

    Memory Trace — Memory Drawing Game

    Free browser game · 2–6 players on one device · English / 繁體中文

    A local 2–6 player memory drawing game. Everyone sees the same shape for three seconds, then redraws it in the same place.

    考你記憶：2–6 人記憶畫圖遊戲。每人私下看同一圖形三秒，圖形消失後在原位重畫；全部完成後，比較重疊率。

    How to play

    1. Choose the players and pass the device to the named person.
    2. Press ready, study the shape for three seconds and redraw it after it disappears.
    3. Submit and pass the device; reveal overlap scores after everyone finishes.

    Play in a modern phone or desktop browser with JavaScript enabled. No account or app download is required. Choose 中 / EN on the playground for Traditional Chinese or English.
