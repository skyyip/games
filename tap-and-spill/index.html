Tap & Spill — Local Pouring Party Game

Free browser game · 2–6 players on one device · English / 繁體中文

A 2–6 player party game on one shared device. Players take turns pouring into the same glass; whoever overflows it loses.

水滿則溢：供 2–6 人共用一部裝置的倒水派對遊戲。輪流控制水龍頭往同一隻杯倒水，令杯中水溢出的玩家落敗。

How to play

  1. Choose two to six players and start the game.
  2. Take turns holding the pour control, then release to end your pour.
  3. Pass the device and avoid being the player who overflows the glass.

Play in a modern phone or desktop browser with JavaScript enabled. No account or app download is required. Choose 中 / EN on the playground for Traditional Chinese or English.
