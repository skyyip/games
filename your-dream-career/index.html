Your Dream Career — Comic Career Experience Game

Free browser game · 1 player · English / 繁體中文

A browser-local career simulation with 405 occupations, practical work puzzles, mentor support, tool choices, workplace topic dilemmas, gradual skill growth and an age-65 retirement ending. Online community features require separate Firebase setup.

我的夢想職涯：專注職業體驗的漫畫模擬遊戲。405種職業、受督導學徒訓練、具體工作難題、工具卡與同事接力；每月安排工作與休整，技能循序成長，保留轉職並在65歲退休。真人好友、公司及委託待獨立Firebase設定後開放。

How to play

  1. Name your character and choose a career. Read its actual work, training requirements and salary reference notes.
  2. Solve hands-on work tasks, ask your mentor, choose an approach and use optional tools. Spend two monthly arrangements on practice, recovery, career changes or workplace dilemmas; decisions leave follow-up records.
  3. Grow skills, qualify and progress, switch careers while keeping experience, and retire at 65. Export your career comic or an independent JSON save.

Play in a modern phone or desktop browser with JavaScript enabled. No account or app download is required. Choose 中 / EN on the playground for Traditional Chinese or English.
