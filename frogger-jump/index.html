        About Frogger Jump 2.5D / 玩法介紹

        Frogger Jump 2.5D — Isometric Frog Jumping Game

        Free browser game · 1 player · English / 繁體中文

        A solo isometric jumping game with wood and lily platforms. Charge a tapered power meter, release to hop, earn golden Frog Coins and use a species-specific skill. Firebase rankings activate after project setup.

        蓄力連連跳：單指蓄力的 2.5D 無盡跳躍遊戲。長按蓄力，放手自動瞄準木樁或睡蓮；落地賺青蛙幣解鎖企鵝、兔子、猴子、水獺、小鴨、花貓、熊貓、狐狸、水豚、糯糯星鼠及小可愛、小八、烏薩奇與島嶼夥伴等漫畫角色，每 20 跳補充一次技能。Firebase 設定完成後可保留唯一名字，參加顯示所用動物的世界前十名排行。

        How to play

        1. Enter a player name, choose an owned animal, then hold the screen, mouse button or Space and release to hop.
        2. Read three wood or lily platforms ahead. Use your animal skill to clear obstacles or improve a landing; another use is earned every 20 successful landings.
        3. Complete a run to earn Frog Coins and unlock more animals. When Firebase is configured, sign in for unique names, cross-device wallets and the public top ten; local practice stays unranked.

        Play in a modern phone or desktop browser with JavaScript enabled. No account or app download is required. Choose 中 / EN on the playground for Traditional Chinese or English.
