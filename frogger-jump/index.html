      About Frogger Jump 2.5D / 玩法介紹

      Frogger Jump 2.5D — Isometric Frog Jumping Game

      Free browser game · 1 player · English / 繁體中文

      A solo isometric jumping game. Charge a ping-pong power meter and release to hop automatically towards the next log. Land accurately to keep your streak alive.

      蓄力蛙：鱷口求生：單指蓄力的 2.5D 無盡跳躍遊戲。長按讓力度在 0–100% 來回，放手自動瞄準下一塊樹樁；預判 I 字與 Z 字路線，避開 2.5 秒沉木陷阱，挑戰最高連跳。

      How to play

      1. Hold the screen, mouse button or Space to charge. Release to jump towards the next stump.
      2. Judge the changing gap and top radius. Read the current log and two upcoming logs as straight and zigzag routes alternate.
      3. From jump 11, leave dark sinking logs within 2.5 seconds of landing. A fall brings out the crocodile; compare your locally saved best runs.

      Play in a modern phone or desktop browser with JavaScript enabled. No account or app download is required. Choose 中 / EN on the playground for Traditional Chinese or English.
