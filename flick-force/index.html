    About Flick Force / 玩法介紹

    Flick Force — Swipe Distance Game

    Free browser game · 1 player · English / 繁體中文

    A solo one-swipe distance game. Your swipe launches a toy car along a runway; the distance is measured in game metres.

    斗指力：單人滑動距離遊戲。在跑道向上或向下滑一次並在移動中放手，看玩具車滑行，挑戰本機最遠遊戲距離。

    How to play

    1. Swipe up or down on the runway, or drag with a mouse.
    2. Release while still moving to launch the car.
    3. Wait for the car to stop and compare the distance with your three best flicks.

    Play in a modern phone or desktop browser with JavaScript enabled. No account or app download is required. Choose 中 / EN on the playground for Traditional Chinese or English.
