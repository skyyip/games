Mo Lei Tau Studio — Cute Hong Kong Comedy Film Studio Game

Free browser game · 1 player · English / 繁體中文

A local-first comedy studio game. Pick one of three movie worlds, reveal three free unique contracts, develop actors and fund a six-person production with clearly labelled local extras.

星爺宇宙：無厘頭片場：可愛 Q 版港式片場養成遊戲。選片、三次免費試鏡、打工及演藝修煉；招募本機街坊臨演，湊齊六人與資金自動開拍，經歷三場危機後保存電影海報。

How to play

  1. Choose a movie and open three free, non-repeating character contracts. Select one as your active actor.
  2. Earn two currencies through timed jobs and reaction or rhythm training. Collect the actor’s three props to awaken their portrait.
  3. Claim a role, recruit local extras and fund the production. Invite a cross-movie guest before filming, then review three event reports, your box-office score and a downloadable poster.

Play in a modern phone or desktop browser with JavaScript enabled. No account or app download is required. Choose 中 / EN on the playground for Traditional Chinese or English.
