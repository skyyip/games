# Stall and kit expansion

All prices are fictional oil-money amounts. Existing catalogue values and saves stay intact. The 30 new kits follow a strictly increasing price/score ladder, with household props first, martial-arts and film novelties next, and rare treasures last. Merit unlocks a kit without being spent; oil buys one ritual use. The 3T ×10 and Mark Six ×15 bonuses remain unchanged. Rebirth paper is now the penultimate kit at 18,000 merit / 18,000 oil / ×28, between Lassie and the immunity token.

## New decorations (base-price order)

Food starts at street-snack prices; larger displays, intricate paper crafts and rare furniture cost more. Base hourly income targets roughly 500–580 active earning hours to recover the initial price, before maintenance and the eight-hour offline cap. These are optional long-term collectibles, not a promise of quick profit. Existing first-level income is preserved. Each later level still adds 0.1/hour, upgrades cost base × next level, and durability lasts 30 real days. Newly bought pieces appear on extra three-column stall shelves immediately.
