    THE GAME PLAN +
    1. Read the growth stage. Normal: fling up. Bud: double-tap. Flower: hold still for 0.6s, then fling up. Every throw finds the mouth.
    2. Make a chain. Short press any Pikmax for 0.2s until it highlights. Keep pressed and drag through others of the same colour and stage, then finish with an upward flick to feed them together. Release without a flick to cancel. A single flower still needs a full 0.6s hold.
    3. Watch them grow. Challenge and 35s Rush change states every 3–8s: 1–5 buds bloom, 1–5 flowers return to normal, and 1–10 normal Pikmax become buds. Held, linked or flying Pikmax are protected.
    4. Choose your finish. Normal and Challenge keep your fastest three clean-ups. In 35s Rush, feed as many as you can before 35 seconds: normal = 1 point, bud = 2, flower = 3. Points count on arrival. Clear all 35 to finish early. Your highest three scores are saved separately.

    About Pikmax Fling / 玩法介紹

    Pikmax Fling — Flinging Arcade Game

    Free browser game · 1 player · English / 繁體中文

    A single-player garden arcade game. Feed a hungry plant by flinging the wandering Pikmax using their gesture prompts.

    皮克鈍投投樂：單人花園街機遊戲。跟著角色的手勢提示，把皮克鈍投入大口植物，挑戰普通、挑戰或 35 秒衝刺模式。

    How to play

    1. Choose Normal, Challenge or 35s Rush.
    2. Follow the characters’ gesture badges and fling them towards the plant.
    3. Clear the crowd or score before the rush timer ends.

    Play in a modern phone or desktop browser with JavaScript enabled. No account or app download is required. Choose 中 / EN on the playground for Traditional Chinese or English.
