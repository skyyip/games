Free browser games for quick breaks and shared screens

Sky Playground is a collection of free games by Sky Yip. Play solo arcade challenges and puzzles, or pass one device between friends for a local party game. No account or app download is needed.

Questions about playing

What is Sky Playground?

Sky Playground is a collection of free browser games by Sky Yip, including solo arcade challenges, puzzles and local party games.

Do I need to download an app or create an account?

No account or app download is needed to play. Open a game in a modern browser and enable JavaScript.

Which games can friends play together?

Tap & Spill, Line Up, Just a Sec, Memory Trace, Dice & Delight and Pocket Poker support 2–6 people sharing one device. Mahjong Match is for two players. These are local games, with no online matchmaking.

Can I play on a phone or desktop?

The games work in modern phone and desktop browsers. Motion-based games provide alternative controls; Flick Force uses a touch swipe or mouse drag.

Which languages are available?

Use 中 / EN on the playground to choose Traditional Chinese or English. The selection carries into the games.
