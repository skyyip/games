      About Roach Rush / 玩法介紹

      Roach Rush — Bug Catching Arcade Game

      Free browser game · 1 player · English / 繁體中文

      A solo timed arcade game with different gestures for ground roaches, flying roaches and mice. Keep the ant friends safe.

      殲滅小強，人人有責：單人限時街機遊戲。點擊地上小強、滑走飛行小強、按住老鼠再拖入籠，並避開螞蟻朋友。

      How to play

      1. Choose a two- or three-minute round.
      2. Tap small ground roaches, double-tap big ones and swipe flying roaches.
      3. Hold a mouse before dragging it to the cage; avoid harming ants.

      Play in a modern phone or desktop browser with JavaScript enabled. No account or app download is required. Choose 中 / EN on the playground for Traditional Chinese or English.
