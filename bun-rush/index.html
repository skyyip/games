    A little guide to buns and parades
    1. The climber goes up automatically for 60 seconds. Drag sideways, hold the arrows, or use Left/Right or A/D to steer.
    2. Only nearby buns with a bright ring can be collected. Each bun can be taken once. The four everyday wishes give one gold blessing; the four extra-lucky wishes give two.
    3. Avoid wet bamboo and other climbers. A safety rope catches a slip, but you lose a little height and climbing time. Your collected blessings stay safe.
    4. After a full round, your points become spendable gold blessings. Your three highest scores are saved on this device. Leaving early gives no reward.
    5. Buy and equip little performers and costume props, then start an 18-second floating-colour parade. Tap six neighbours once each for extra laughs. Laughs and good fortune are saved separately from gold blessings.
    6. Pause whenever you need. Hiding the page pauses both games and their sounds.

    Inspired by Cheung Chau’s Tai Ping Ching Chiu bun scrambling and floating-colour procession. These playful costumes turn everyday worries into a little island humour.

    Sea waves, ferry horns and stone-step sounds are locally synthesized. Sound begins after your first interaction.

    Cheung Chau Bun Rush — Bun Tower Game

    Free browser game · 1 player · English / 繁體中文

    A single-player game inspired by Cheung Chau’s bun festival, combining a sixty-second bun tower climb with a little harbour parade.

    登頂啦！平安包：以長洲包山節慶為靈感的單人遊戲。挑戰六十秒搶包山、收集平安包與願望，再為小巡遊裝扮。

    How to play

    1. Start the sixty-second bun tower challenge.
    2. Follow the game prompts to climb and collect buns.
    3. Explore the wishes and dress the harbour parade performers.

    Play in a modern phone or desktop browser with JavaScript enabled. No account or app download is required. Choose 中 / EN on the playground for Traditional Chinese or English.
