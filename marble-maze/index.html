    About Marble & Mist / 玩法介紹

    Marble & Mist — Tilt Maze Game

    Free browser game · 1 player · English / 繁體中文

    A solo marble maze game with five layouts. Reach the finish while dealing with mist and moving bombs.

    波珠走迷宮：單人波珠迷宮遊戲。傾斜手機或使用觸控及鍵盤，把波珠送到終點；連續兩次搖動或按清霧控制來驅霧，並避開炸彈。

    How to play

    1. Choose a maze and phone or touch controls.
    2. Roll from the start to the finish without touching a bomb.
    3. Use two shakes or the equivalent fallback control to clear mist.

    Play in a modern phone or desktop browser with JavaScript enabled. No account or app download is required. Choose 中 / EN on the playground for Traditional Chinese or English.
