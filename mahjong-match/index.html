The little mahjong rules

One full little table

  1. Two players share all 144 tiles: 108 numbered tiles, 16 winds, 12 dragons, four seasons and four flowers. Numbered tiles and honours have four copies each; each season and flower appears once.
  2. One shuffled tile opens as the target. The other 143 stay face down. The target rank stays fixed for the entire round.
  3. Player 1 can reveal immediately. Each safe reveal automatically starts the other player’s turn. Revealed tiles stay visible and cannot be selected again.

Tile ranks: different faces can match

Compare the rank, not the suit or exact symbol. One Character, one Circle, one Bamboo, East, Red, Spring and Plum all count as rank 1. Use the full table below.

Alternating turns

Reveal the same rank as the target: you are safe and play passes to the other player. Reveal a different rank: you lose and the other player wins. If Player 1 misses immediately, Player 2 wins.

Sudden death

Reveal a different rank: you are safe and play passes to the other player. Reveal the target rank: you lose immediately and the other player wins. Different faces at the same rank still trigger sudden death.

A fresh little chance

The selected mode stays fixed during play. A new round keeps the names and mode, shuffles all 144 tiles and reveals a fresh target.
