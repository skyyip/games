About 365 Careers, One Little Life / 玩法介紹

365 Careers, One Little Life — Comic Life and Career Simulation

Free browser game · 1 player · English / 繁體中文

A single-player, browser-local life simulation with 365 distinct careers, monthly actions, mentor and simulated AI learning, fictional investments and gambling, and a personal comic book ending.

365行，行行出狀元：以柔和漫畫分鏡記錄人生的單人模擬遊戲。18–26歲隨機履歷開局，從365種職業中選擇拜師或模擬AI自學，安排每月行動、經營事業、轉行與虛擬理財，65歲退休並下載完整人生繪本。

How to play

  1. Draw a resume, name your protagonist and choose one of 365 careers with a mentor or simulated AI learning route.
  2. Spend three monthly actions on learning, work, rest, relationships or business. Manage fictional assets and resolve story choices; yearly plans stop at decisions.
  3. Retire at 65, review achievements and locally assembled reflections, and export a complete offline comic book, PNG highlights or a JSON save.

Play in a modern phone or desktop browser with JavaScript enabled. No account or app download is required. Choose 中 / EN on the playground for Traditional Chinese or English.
