    Every little dice rule

    Take turns

    1. Choose 2–6 players. For highest or lowest total, choose 1–5 dice. Five-dice poker always uses exactly five dice.
    2. Pass the phone to the named player and tap My turn. Shake the phone or press Shake the dice. Each player gets one complete roll.
    3. Phone motion only starts a roll. A stronger shake does not change the result or improve the odds. All six faces are equally likely.
    4. A roll cannot be changed, held or repeated. After everyone rolls, compare the results using the rule chosen before the round.

    Highest or lowest total

    Highest total: add all dice and the biggest sum wins. Lowest total: the smallest sum wins. Equal sums share the win, even when the individual dice differ.

    Five-dice poker: strongest to weakest

    Compare the hand type first. The table is ordered strongest to weakest. A five-dice straight ranks above three of a kind and below a full house. Dice have no suits, so there are no flushes or straight flushes.

    HandExampleIf the hand type matches
    Five of a kind6 · 6 · 6 · 6 · 6Five equal dice. Compare the repeated face; five sixes beat five fives.
    Four of a kind3 · 3 · 3 · 3 · 6Four equal dice. Compare the four first, then the remaining die. Four threes beat three fours.
    Full house5 · 5 · 5 · 2 · 2A triple and a pair. Compare the triple first, then the pair.
    Straight (five dice)2 · 3 · 4 · 5 · 6Five consecutive faces. Only 1–2–3–4–5 and 2–3–4–5–6 count. The straight ending in six wins; there is no wraparound.
    Three of a kind5 · 5 · 5 · 2 · 1Three equal dice, without a pair. Compare the triple, then the two remaining dice from high to low. Three fives beat three fours.
    Two pairs6 · 6 · 4 · 4 · 2Two separate pairs. Compare the higher pair, then the lower pair, then the last die.
    One pair6 · 6 · 5 · 3 · 1One pair. Compare the pair first, then the remaining dice from high to low. Two sixes beat two fives.
    High dice1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 6No pair or straight. Sort the five faces from high to low and compare one at a time.

    A lovely shared win

    If every comparison is equal, players share the win. Roll order never breaks a tie. A new round gives everyone a fresh roll.

    Phone shake and privacy

    Motion permission may be requested on supported phones. If it is denied, unavailable or no readings arrive, the roll button works normally. Motion readings stay on this device.
