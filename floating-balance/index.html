    About Float & Balance / 玩法介紹

    Float & Balance — Marble Balance Game

    Free browser game · 1 player · English / 繁體中文

    A solo balance challenge. Keep a marble on a floating platform for as long as possible while disturbances change its motion.

    保持平衡力：單人波珠平衡挑戰。在圓形、正方形或三角形浮台上保持波珠不掉落，同時應付風、地震及掉落包裹。

    How to play

    1. Choose a platform shape and phone tilt or touch controls.
    2. Tilt or steer gently to keep the marble inside the platform edge.
    3. Respond to disturbance warnings and beat your survival time for that shape.

    Play in a modern phone or desktop browser with JavaScript enabled. No account or app download is required. Choose 中 / EN on the playground for Traditional Chinese or English.
