      About Just a Sec / 玩法介紹

      Just a Sec — Time Perception Party Game

      Free browser game · 2–6 players on one device · English / 繁體中文

      A 2–6 player local timing game. Estimate a shared target of 1–20 seconds without a visible running timer.

      時間感挑戰：2–6 人時間感挑戰。憑感覺按住按鈕 1–20 秒的指定時間，放手鎖定答案，最接近目標時間的玩家勝出。

      How to play

      1. Choose the players and reveal the shared target duration.
      2. Each player holds the circle and releases when the target time feels complete.
      3. After all turns, reveal the ranking by distance from the target time.

      Play in a modern phone or desktop browser with JavaScript enabled. No account or app download is required. Choose 中 / EN on the playground for Traditional Chinese or English.
