About The City is Dying / 玩法介紹

The City is Dying — Hong Kong Psychological Survival RPG

Free browser game · 1 player · English / 繁體中文

A local-first solo narrative RPG with snowbound supply decisions, three city viewpoints, linked social pressures, 54 memory fragments, original rhythm or untimed music and eight endings.

天與地：The City is Dying：非官方原創互動演繹：雪山物資抉擇、三位主角的城市行動、罪疚與理智管理、54 件碎片及最後合奏，導向八個結局。本機自動存檔與三個手動槽，可匯出與匯入。

How to play

  1. Share scarce supplies and make six nights of decisions, including responsibility for a terrible dinner.
  2. Switch freely between three city viewpoints sharing three actions a day. Balance money, prices, solidarity, trust and a growing investigation; reconstruct memories and rehearse.
  3. Arrange a final stage and choose what to reveal. Play original music using rhythm, slow or untimed controls, then discover an ending and replay using local save slots.

Play in a modern phone or desktop browser with JavaScript enabled. No account or app download is required. Choose 中 / EN on the playground for Traditional Chinese or English.
