      About Pot Luck / 玩法介紹

      Pot Luck — Arrow Tossing Game

      Free browser game · 1 player · English / 繁體中文

      A solo courtyard tossing game. Land arrows inside a moving chamber pot: bamboo arrows earn one point and rainbow arrows earn two.

      拋箭入尿壺：單人投箭遊戲。在兩分鐘或三分鐘內，把竹箭和彩虹箭拋入會移動的尿壺；竹箭得 1 分，彩虹箭得 2 分。

      How to play

      1. Choose a two- or three-minute round.
      2. Swipe upward from the arrow or use mouse drag; keyboard charging is also available.
      3. Adjust aim and strength to land in the opening as the pot begins moving.

      Play in a modern phone or desktop browser with JavaScript enabled. No account or app download is required. Choose 中 / EN on the playground for Traditional Chinese or English.
